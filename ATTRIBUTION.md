## Animation works (2.0)

The eighteen animation scenes study posts by their artists, linked from each scene, from exported pages and code, and listed with their dates in `docs/WORKS.md`:

- **yonatan (@zozuar)**: nine twigl.app shaders (Jellyfish lattice, Stormy sea, Blossoming tree, Folded jewel box, Lace garden, Carved stone kaleidoscope, Underwater reef, Golden spiral, Rainbow trefoil).
- **Xor (@XorDev)**: “Vortex”, a twigl.app shader.
- **Yohei Nishitsuji (@YoheiNishitsuji)**: three twigl.app shaders (Cloud cave, Smoke tunnel, Cloud mountains).
- **ア (@yuruyurau)**: four p5.js point sketches (Point jellyfish, Swimming creature, Circling twins, Circling twins, bristled).
- **Jae (@Jaenam97)**: “Fluffy Anemone”. Its shader was not published; the scene is Equation Studio’s own study of the look and is labelled as such.

Seventeen scenes run the code their artists published in the posts, unchanged, next to a readable version written for this project; the titles, except “Vortex” and “Fluffy Anemone”, are descriptive titles given by Equation Studio. The artists’ code remains theirs: it is reproduced with credit for study and explanation, and the MIT license of this repository does not apply to it. twigl.app’s helper functions (`src/twigl-glsl.js`) are by doxas (MIT License) and include Ashima Arts / Stefan Gustavson’s simplex noise (MIT License). No clip or image from the posts is embedded; thumbnails are rendered by the studio.
