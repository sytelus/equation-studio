START HERE
Think in fields. Build in layers.
01 / Find your way
The top bar names the scene; ☰ Scenes opens the library. The Pipeline under the canvas lists the construction step by step with a live picture of each output: click a step to select it, and the panel on the right explains it ([ ] or ◀ ▶ move to the previous or next step). Above the canvas you choose what it shows: the Final image, This step (the selected component’s own output) or What it changes. A label on the canvas always says which.
02 / Understand a component
The panel’s tabs: Equation shows it as numbered steps, each explained, with colored inputs, parameters and output, then its parameters and key function. In & out shows where its values come from and what its output becomes downstream. Ideas explains the ideas behind it with plots. ⤢ Playground (E, or double-click a step) widens the panel and plots the actual values under the canvas; ↗ pops it out.
03 / Edit an equation
Press ✎ Edit on the equation (or double-click it). It opens as text, one line per step: param k = 1 [0, 2] makes a slider, d = length(p) - 1 a definition, // … a caption, and the last line is the result. The canvas previews your edit, marked DRAFT; Apply (Ctrl/⌘ Enter) puts it into the scene and Cancel discards it. Built-in components open as their own math written out, so you change what you read.
04 / Experiment and compose
Drag a parameter symbol in an equation, or a slider; ↺ resets, ▦ renders the image across the whole range, ✦ Variations suggests nearby versions. Hold ◐ Original (O) to compare, ⟲ Revert to start over; Undo always works. Untick a step to bypass it. ＋ Component adds building blocks; in the Function graph, drag between dots to wire them.
Is the image live? Yes. Every pixel is computed on your GPU from the equations each time anything changes; the LIVE GPU badge pulses on each frame and says whether hardware acceleration is on (click it for details). Changing the wiring or an equation compiles a new shader in the background while the last image stays up. Only the small scene thumbnails in the library are saved pictures. The nebula ports the supplied equations to GPU float32; the other scenes are original interpretations, not recovered artist formulas: click the provenance label next to the title.
Keys: Space play · Home/End start/end · , . step a frame · [ ] previous/next component · I this step · C what it changes · E Playground · V profile · Esc close / back to the final image · hold O original · P previews · R rulers · G grid · F fit · S snapshot · L locate · Ctrl/⌘ Z undo · Ctrl/⌘ Shift Z redo · Ctrl/⌘ S save · Ctrl/⌘ D duplicate · Ctrl/⌘ Enter apply an equation · Delete delete (graph focused)
Canvas: drag to pan, wheel or pinch to zoom about the cursor, click to pin a reading, Alt-click for a one-off raw value. Equations: drag a parameter symbol sideways to change it (Shift for fine steps). On touch screens, press and hold a control for its explanation. A saved JSON contains the graph, equations, colors, parameters, camera and keys. Reference images and snapshots stay in this browser.

Export your construction
PNG exports the exact canvas samples at the selected resolution. Preview width does not reduce equation terms.
PNG sequences are deterministic at t = frame / FPS and include the project and manifest. Video records in real time: each frame is drawn at its exact time, but a slow device skips frames. End-of-loop continuity depends on your equations and keys. Exports use no online service.
