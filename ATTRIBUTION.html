Attribution and provenance

The supplied Bipolar Nebula mathematical artwork is credited to Hamid Naderi Yeganeh. The prior Python project, its formula transcription, explanatory documentation, tests and generated reference images are retained under reference/nebula_rewrite/. The new GPU port follows that supplied mathematical specification. It is a port/refactoring, not the artist’s recovered original program.

The five linked newer subjects are represented by independent interpretive procedural studies. The complete corresponding formula sheets were not recovered. Hedgehog and Fire are original teaching examples, not transcriptions of videos that were not inspected. Do not present these new formulas as the artist’s own equations or as exact reconstructions of those posts.

This application is independent and is not affiliated with, sponsored by, or endorsed by the artist. The sources are linked and their recovery status is recorded in docs/RESEARCH.md and the application’s Research panel. No external source image, artist thumbnail, downloaded video, or photographic texture is embedded in the new application. Its scene thumbnails and illustrations are outputs of its own renderer. The retained source-reference reconstruction remains attributed to the supplied artwork.

The software permission in LICENSE applies to newly authored Equation Studio software and documentation. It makes no claim to rights in the artist’s underlying artworks, external publications or third-party materials. The original project’s attribution file is retained. External publications are linked, not reproduced wholesale.

Animation works (2.0)

The eighteen animation scenes study posts by their artists, linked from each scene, from exported pages and code, and listed with their dates in docs/WORKS.md:

  • yonatan (@zozuar): nine twigl.app shaders (Jellyfish lattice, Stormy sea, Blossoming tree, Folded jewel box, Lace garden, Carved stone kaleidoscope, Underwater reef, Golden spiral, Rainbow trefoil).
  • Xor (@XorDev): “Vortex”, a twigl.app shader.
  • Yohei Nishitsuji (@YoheiNishitsuji): three twigl.app shaders (Cloud cave, Smoke tunnel, Cloud mountains).
  • ア (@yuruyurau): four p5.js point sketches (Point jellyfish, Swimming creature, Circling twins, Circling twins, bristled).
  • Jae (@Jaenam97): “Fluffy Anemone”. Its shader was not published; the scene is Equation Studio’s own study of the look and is labelled as such.

Seventeen scenes run the code their artists published in the posts, unchanged, next to a readable version written for this project; the titles, except “Vortex” and “Fluffy Anemone”, are descriptive titles given by Equation Studio. The artists’ code remains theirs: it is reproduced with credit for study and explanation, and the MIT license of this repository does not apply to it. twigl.app’s helper functions (src/twigl-glsl.js) are by doxas (MIT License) and include Ashima Arts / Stefan Gustavson’s simplex noise (MIT License). No clip or image from the posts is embedded; thumbnails are rendered by the studio.
