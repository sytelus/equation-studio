What this package adds

This package adds a newly structured NumPy implementation, semantic function names, configuration objects, numerical safeguards, documentation, independent scalar-reference tests, a tiled renderer, an offline explanatory viewer, and new composition examples.

Descriptions such as “turbulence,” “first-hit selection,” “rim,” “filament,” and “star lattice” are computational/visual interpretations of the formulas, supported by isolating their outputs. They are not quotations of the artist's own design notes, claims that the image is physically simulated, or an assertion of having invented the original construction.

The bundled reconstruction and original-parameter layer renders are outputs of the supplied artistic formulas. The twin-nebula and elliptical-ring images are new compositions using the rewritten components. The supplied JPEG itself is not bundled; its checksum and comparison measurements may appear in the validation report.

No ownership or license over the artist's original image or artistic expression is asserted by this package. Attribution here is not a substitute for permission to redistribute or commercially exploit the original artwork. The code and explanations are provided as the requested implementation/refactoring; no third-party artwork is relabeled as newly authored original work.
